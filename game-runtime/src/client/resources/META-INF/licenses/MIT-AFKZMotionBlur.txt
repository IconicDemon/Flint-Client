AFKZ Motion Blur 1.0 by Neerajisafk (MIT, as its fabric.mod.json states; the jar carries no licence text, so this is the standard
MIT text). Flint's Motion Blur Smooth mode (control/fx/BlurRules.java and each line's control/fx/MotionBlur.java) adapts its
temporal blend of the last blended frame into the new one, its default strength and its history resets.
